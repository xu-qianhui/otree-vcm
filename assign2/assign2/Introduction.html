Overview

This study has {{ num_rounds }} rounds. In every round you are in a group of {{ group_size }} participants. You will not learn the identity of the other members of your group, and they will not learn yours.

At the start of each round, every group member receives an endowment of {{ endowment }} points. You decide how many of these points (any whole number from 0 to {{ endowment }}) to contribute to a group project. Points you do not contribute are kept in your private account.

How your earnings are calculated
  • Every point you keep in your private account earns 1 point for you only.
  • The group project pays every group member the total contribution of the group multiplied by a number called the MPCR (marginal per capita return). Every member receives this amount, whether or not they contributed.
Your earnings = ({{ endowment }} − your contribution) + MPCR × (total contribution of your group)
